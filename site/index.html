ScratchPad

A lightweight tabbed notepad.
Quick notes, stored locally — nothing leaves your computer.

⬇ Download for Mac

macOS · Apple Silicon · v1.1.0

After dragging to Applications, macOS may block the app.
Open Terminal and run:

xattr -cr /Applications/ScratchPad.app

Then open the app normally.
⬇ Download for Windows

Windows 10/11 · x64 · v1.0.0

  • Tabbed notepads
  • Privacy mode — hide note contents
  • Always on top
  • Adjustable opacity
  • Dark & light theme
  • Auto-saves while you type
  • Minimises to system tray
